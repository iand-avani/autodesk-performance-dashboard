        Methodology

        Account normalization: “Microsoft Corporation” is consolidated into “Microsoft.”

        Director+: Director, VP, and C-level records.

        Persona classification: Rules-based clustering using job-title keywords into technology, construction, facilities, engineering, sustainability, and other enterprise roles.

        Asset analysis: Counts reflect delivered records mapped to each touch and asset.

        Forecast methodology

        The scenario planner divides cumulative delivered volume by the editable historical flight days, then applies future flight length, optimization uplift, rejection rate, and delivery-capacity assumptions. Conservative and upside cases are shown at 90% and 110% of the base projection. This is a transparent scenario model—not a time-series forecast—because the source file does not include delivery dates.
